    What the power costs

    No microreactor has sold power yet, so every number here is an estimate. The bands are wide because sources disagree about what the first units will cost to build.

    Use this chart to rule markets out, not to set a price. First-unit estimates overlap expensive diesel; they do not compete with ordinary grid power. The Alaska case is shown separately because it is an unusually high-cost diesel system, not a general tariff.

    Units: source-reported currency per megawatt-hour. U.S. dollars marks sources that state the currency; source $ means the source uses a dollar sign but does not identify the currency. Dollar years are not published or normalized, so compare scale, not exchange-rate precision. Levelized cost of electricity (LCOE) spreads estimated lifetime cost across estimated lifetime electricity output. First-of-a-kind (FOAK) is the first commercial build; nth-of-a-kind (NOAK) is a later factory-series build after learning and repetition.
