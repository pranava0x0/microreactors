About this site

A map of where a 1–20 MW reactor could actually be sold: who is buying, what the power would have to cost, which rules open or block the sale, and how a deal like that gets signed. It is built from public documents: regulator filings, federal award records, utility tariffs and company disclosures.

A field reading not found means the research did not find it. Blanks are left blank.
